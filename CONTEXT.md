# Hellward language

**Arrow Tower** — the plain, single-target tower that anchors the opening economy.

**Breach** — an optional side entrance and its harder enemy pack, joined to an announced wave in exchange for a chosen reward if the pack is defeated.

**Gold** — currency earned and spent within one defence; it does not carry to the next.

**Salvage** — an item dropped by an ordinary enemy. It can be sold for gold in the current defence or banked after a victory for forging.

**Trophy** — a unique item from a defeated breach elite. A trophy can help forge a powerful pattern instead of taking the breach's immediate gold cache.

**Pattern** — a forged modifier equipped to one tower family before a defence. It affects every tower of that family built in the fight.

**Route** — a committed trail from one entrance to the sanctuary. A monster follows one route for its whole crossing.

**Wanderer** — a monster that may enter on one of several bounded detour routes.

**Runner** — a monster that takes the shortest route toward the sanctuary.

**Sigil** — the existing reward for a location victory, spent on skills and tower-rank unlocks.
