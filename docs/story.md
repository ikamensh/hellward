# Hellward — the story

The words of the campaign. [Campaign](campaign.md) says where each page appears. The prologue is the intro comic
([intro](intro.md)). Every other page lives in `hellward/story.py`: one painted panel, a few lines, and what the
panel shows. The Bone Priest's taunts are on the locations (`hellward/sim/campaign.py`).

## Who is in it

- **You:** the keeper, the one the old towers answer. You are never named, and never seen closer than a hooded
  shadow with a lantern.
- **The Bone Priest:** the cathedral's priest. He kept its lamp for nine hundred years and is dead and not gone. He
  narrates the prologue, taunts on every intro, and fights you himself at the very end.
- **His acolytes:** the Bone Acolytes that walk with the monsters. They cast the curses his bones chose.
- **Akara:** a priestess of the Sisterhood. She finds you in burning Tristram and tells you what lies ahead. At the
  end of Act I she stays behind to keep the lamp. Her blessing is the free unlearning in the tree.
- **The necromancer:** a Priest of Rathma, pale and exact, who meets you at the Kurast Docks. The Bone Priest stole
  his order's art, and he lends it back to you as the Bone Altar.
- **The eldest druid:** the keeper of the oak ring in the Spider Forest. The oaks remember the saints who lit the
  lamps. He brings the Grove, and at the end the oil that relights the mother lamp.
- **The lamps:** the saints lit a lamp over every door to hell, all from one flame: the mother lamp in the Temple of
  Light at Kurast. If it goes out, every lamp lit from it goes out. Your lantern carries Tristram's flame. That is why
  the old towers answer you abroad, and why in Act II the sanctuary orb is the lantern's flame.

## The rules the words keep

- **The bones:** they show the Bone Priest every stone, tower and demon of a fight, and never you. This is the game's
  own rule, since a leader's rollouts see the towers but never the player's hand. His certainty, his wait at the
  door, his flight east and his coming to look at you himself all follow from it.
- **His arc** runs through the taunts, one per location: certain, then annoyed, then curious, then afraid, then
  bargaining. The act endings show the turn.
- **"Your saints":** only the Bone Priest says it. It is his tell, as in the prologue.
- **Tense:** before pages are in the present tense and after pages in the past.
- **Length:** at most three short paragraphs a page, readable in the pause between two fights.
- **Painting:** the panel bible is `REFERENCES`, `STYLES` and `RULES` in `hellward/story.py`.
  - **References:** the recurring figures and objects are painted once as references and passed with every panel
    they appear in. So is the location's painted floor.
  - **Composition:** one subject per panel. The lower quarter is dark, since the text sits there.
  - **No writing:** a letter is folded paper with a broken seal.
  - **Violet** glow is his sight.
  - **Palette:** Act I is bone, rust and dried blood; Act II is moss, black water and tarnished gilt.

## The taunts

| Location | The Bone Priest |
|---|---|
| Tristram | Huddle your towers together, if it comforts you. When my shaman's curse falls, it falls on all of them. |
| The Graveyard | I buried every one of them, and they still come when I call. Build your gates. My acolytes will cage the fire behind them. |
| The Cathedral | Two nights you have kept my lamp. It changes nothing. The witch will make your towers old, and the goatmen do not fear your thunder. |
| The Catacombs | Four doors between you and the dark. I have watched the Overlords break every one of them. Why are you still here? |
| The Caves | Walls mean nothing to wings. Look up. And tell me, keeper: why do my bones never show your face? |
| Hell's Gate | In every night I have seen, the lamp goes out. I have begun to wonder what I have not seen. |
| Kurast Docks | You crossed a sea for a lamp that is not yours. My shaman will raise every Flayer you leave whole. |
| The Spider Forest | The old trees have taken your side. Stand close to them, then. One curse will find you all. |
| The Flayer Jungle | My inquisitors do not chant. You will know their curse when the ground burns under your towers, and not before. |
| The Drowned City | The Hulks were drowned men once. They do not tire, and your gates are cloth to them. |
| Travincal | Turn back, and I will leave Tristram's lamp alone. It is a small lamp. No one would miss it. |
| The Temple of Light | I cannot see you in the bones. So I have come to see you myself. |
