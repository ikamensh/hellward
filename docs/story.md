# Hellward — the story

The campaign's words, page by page. [Campaign](campaign.md) says where each page appears; the prologue is the
intro comic ([intro](intro.md)). Every page has one painted panel, in the prologue's heavy-ink style, and a few
lines. Quoted lines are spoken by a character, and the rest is the chronicle's voice.

## Who is in it

- **You:** the one the old towers answer. Never named, never seen closer than a shadow with a lantern.
- **The Bone Priest:** the cathedral's priest, keeper of its lamp for nine hundred years, dead and not gone. He
  casts bones and sees the fight before it happens, and in every fight he has seen, the lamp goes out. He narrates
  the prologue and taunts on every intro. He is never wrong, until you.
- **Akara:** a priestess of the Sisterhood. She finds you in burning Tristram and tells you what lies ahead. Her
  blessing is the free unlearning in the tree.
- **The necromancer:** a Priest of Rathma, pale and exact, who joins at the Kurast Docks. The Bone Priest stole his
  order's art. He lends it back to you: the Bone Altar.
- **The eldest druid:** the keeper of the grove in the Spider Forest. The oaks there remember the saints who
  planted the lamps. The druids bring the Grove.
- **The lamps:** the saints lit one lamp over every door to hell. Tristram's is one of them. The mother flame hangs
  in the Temple of Light in Kurast, and every other lamp was lit from it.

The Bone Priest's arc: he is certain → annoyed → curious → afraid → defeated, and at the end he sees the one night
he never saw. Every "after" page moves him one step.

## Act I — The Descent

### Tristram

**Before.** *Panel: the village burning at night, the Fallen pouring out of cellars with knives and torches; a
lantern at the well in the foreground.*

> The lamp guttered at midnight, and an hour later Tristram was burning. The Fallen came up through the cellars
> with knives and torches, and behind them walked the village dead, still in their Sunday clothes.
>
> Akara found you at the well. "The old towers answer whoever keeps the lamp," she said. "Keep it tonight. Ask me
> your questions at dawn."

**After.** *Panel: grey dawn over ashes; a hand holding a finger bone carved with a cross.*

> Dawn came grey over the ashes. Among the dead shaman's charms lay a finger bone, carved with the cathedral's
> seal. It was a priest's gift.
>
> The Fallen had not come on their own. Someone had sent them up from the churchyard.

### The Graveyard

**Before.** *Panel: an open churchyard gate, empty graves, and among the headstones the Bone Priest raising his
staff; skeletons climbing out.*

> The churchyard gate stands open and every grave is empty. The Bone Priest walks among the stones, calling the
> buried by name. He christened most of them. He buried all of them.

**After.** *Panel: a crypt wall covered in tally marks, row on row, each crossed through; Akara's hand with a
candle.*

> On the crypt wall someone has scratched a tally: hundreds of marks, row on row, each one crossed through.
>
> Akara held her candle to it and went pale. "He is counting nights. Every night he has cast his bones and watched
> this fight. And every one of them, he won."

### The Cathedral

**Before.** *Panel: the nave, the torn red carpet, goatmen kneeling in the pews, a Blood Witch at the pulpit; the
red lamp high above.*

> The nave is as you dreamed it: the torn carpet, the three arches, the lamp high above. But the goatmen have come
> down from the hills to kneel in the pews, and a Blood Witch sings vespers from the pulpit.
>
> The priest has invited guests.

**After.** *Panel: beneath the altar, a stair going down into darkness; on its first step a guttering candle stub.*

> The lamp still burns. Beneath the altar, a stair you had never seen goes down into cold air, and on its first
> step stands a candle stub, still warm.
>
> He went down ahead of you. He wanted you to follow.

### The Catacombs

**Before.** *Panel: halls of stacked skulls to the ceiling, bones sliding loose; an Overlord's silhouette breaking a
barred door.*

> Under the cathedral lie nine hundred years of the faithful, stacked to the ceiling. Tonight they are unstacking
> themselves. The Overlords came up from somewhere deeper. The priest opened the old doors for them, and taught them
> to break the new ones.

**After.** *Panel: a bare cell: a cot, an unlit lamp, and on the floor bones laid out as a map of rooms, one room
empty.*

> In the deepest ossuary you found his cell: a cot, a cold lamp, and his bones, laid out on the floor in the shape
> of every room you have fought in. One room had no bones on it yet.
>
> Beside it he had scratched three words: *the gate, tonight*.

### The Caves

**Before.** *Panel: a vast cavern lit red from below by a lava lake, gargoyles hanging in the vault like bats.*

> The catacombs open onto a cavern lit from below. Lava crawls across its floor, and gargoyles hang in the vault,
> waiting for the heat to lift them.
>
> Akara will go no further. "Past the lava is the door your saints built on. Past the door there is only him."

**After.** *Panel: steps cut into the rock at the lava's edge, worn into hollows; a single set of footprints.*

> At the lava's edge the rock has been cut into steps, and the steps are worn into hollows by one pair of feet.
>
> Every night for nine hundred years, the keeper of the lamp came down here to look at the door. Somewhere in those
> years, he began to hope it would open.

### Hell's Gate

**Before.** *Panel: a colossal door in the rock, open a hand's width, hellfire through the gap and the shape of
Azazel beyond; the Bone Priest at the threshold, turned toward you.*

> The door your saints built on stands open a hand's width. Through the gap you see fire, and Azazel the Flayer,
> and around him the whole council of curses.
>
> The Bone Priest waits at the threshold. "Nine hundred years I kept the lamp," he says. "Tonight I let it go out."

**After.** *Panel: the door slamming shut on Azazel's fallen body; the Bone Priest staring at bones in his palm
that show nothing.*

> Azazel fell in the doorway, and the door swung shut on him. The Bone Priest looked down at the bones in his hand.
> For the first time in nine hundred years they showed him nothing.
>
> "One night in a thousand," he whispered. "Only one." Then he stepped back into the dark, away from the door,
> east.

### The end of Act I

*Panel 1: the Tristram lamp burning straight and red in the nave, dawn light through the windows.*

> The lamp of Tristram burns.

*Panel 2: a map of the world on old vellum: many small red flames scattered over it, one great flame far east
across the sea.*

> But it was never the only one. The saints lit a lamp over every door to hell, and all of them were lit from one
> flame: the mother lamp, in the Temple of Light at Kurast, across the sea.

*Panel 3: a ship's black sails leaving a harbour at night; a skeletal figure at the stern.*

> Akara's sisters send word from the coast. A ship left harbour last night with no crew anyone saw, and it is
> sailing east.
>
> **End of Act I.**

## Act II — The Drowned Temples

### Kurast Docks

**Before.** *Panel: a harbour city of gold roofs and rotting wood rising from swamp; Flayers crouched along the
piers; a pale man in bone armour on the harbour wall.*

> Kurast rises out of the swamp in tiers of gold and rotting wood. The harbour is empty of pilgrims. Along the piers
> the Flayers are waiting, and their shaman is already singing.
>
> A pale man in bone armour watches you land. "I am a priest of Rathma," he says. "Your enemy stole our art. Let me
> lend it back to you."

**After.** *Panel: the necromancer kneeling among dead Flayers, closing their eyes.*

> The shaman's song stopped. The necromancer knelt among the Flayers and closed their eyes. "He raised them twice,"
> he said. "Each time, less of them came back.
>
> "He is not raising an army. He is spending one."

### The Spider Forest

**Before.** *Panel: webs hung like sails between giant trees, bats pouring out at dusk; in a clearing, a ring of
enormous oaks and a druid with antlered staff.*

> The road to the temples runs through a forest the spiders own. Webs hang between the trees like sails, and at dusk
> the bats pour out of them.
>
> In a clearing stands a ring of oaks older than Kurast. "The trees remember the saints who lit the lamps," says
> the eldest druid. "They will stand with you, if you stand close to them."

**After.** *Panel: the grove at dawn, roots already closing over fallen spiders; the druid pointing east to black
water.*

> The grove held. Where the spiders fell, the oaks were already putting down roots.
>
> The eldest druid pointed east, to where the jungle turns to black water. "He passed here three nights ago. The
> trees did not like him."

### The Flayer Jungle

**Before.** *Panel: a jungle path under a green gloom; zealots in white and gold marching; an inquisitor raising a
hand, a curse already landing without a word.*

> The jungle closes over the path. Zealots of the fallen church march through it in white and gold, and their
> inquisitors curse without a word of prayer.
>
> There will be no chant to warn you, and no time to strike first. Keep your wards ready.

**After.** *Panel: a gloved hand holding a letter sealed with the High Council's seal, the Bone Priest's spidery
hand across it.*

> Among the inquisitors' dead you found the seal of the High Council, and a letter in the Bone Priest's hand: *Your
> lamp gutters, as mine did. Let me keep it for you.*
>
> The Council let him in.

### The Drowned City

**Before.** *Panel: half-sunken temples, streets turned to canals, a Thorned Hulk wading out of the water.*

> Half of Kurast has sunk into the swamp. Its streets are canals and its temples islands, and something huge walks
> the flooded avenues: the Thorned Hulks, grown out of the drowned. They break a gate like a door made of paper.

**After.** *Panel: a flooded shrine, the water warm and red; the necromancer tasting it from his fingers.*

> In a flooded shrine the water was warm and red. The necromancer tasted it and spat. "Lamp oil," he said. "The
> mother lamp's.
>
> "He is not putting it out. He is draining it, a little each night, so no one will notice until it is dark."

### Travincal

**Before.** *Panel: the temple terrace at night, twelve elders in gold robes, each with a curse glowing in his
hand; above them the mother lamp, dim.*

> The High Council meets on the temple terrace under the mother lamp. There are twelve elders in gold, and every
> one of them curses.
>
> They have waited for you. The Bone Priest has told them exactly how you fight.

**After.** *Panel: the last elder fallen on the temple stairs; the temple doors closing by themselves; the dim lamp
visible through the gap.*

> The last elder fell on the temple stairs. Above them the mother lamp hung dim and red, its oil almost gone. The
> temple doors swung shut by themselves.
>
> He was inside, and he was waiting.

### The Temple of Light

**Before.** *Panel: a vast golden temple interior; the mother lamp hanging over an altar; the Bone Priest beneath
it, grown huge, bones in his hand, violet fire around him.*

> Under the mother lamp the Bone Priest waits, and for the first time he will fight you himself.
>
> "I have cast these bones ten thousand times since Tristram," he says. "In every night I have seen since, I win.
> Every night but one."

**After.** *Panel: the Bone Priest's bones scattered across the golden floor, and in their pattern, faintly, a lamp
burning and a figure beside it.*

> The bones fell from his hand and scattered across the floor, and in them, at last, he saw the night he had never
> seen: the lamp burning, and you beside it.
>
> "So this is the one," he said, and was dust.

### The end of Act II

*Panel 1: the necromancer pouring holy oil into the mother lamp; its flame rising gold.*

> The necromancer filled the mother lamp from the last jar of holy oil, and its flame rose gold.

*Panel 2: far across the sea, the Tristram cathedral at dawn; inside, the small red lamp standing straight.*

> Across the sea, in a cathedral built on a door to hell, a small red flame stood straight again.

*Panel 3: black; a lamp's spark.*

> Keep it burning.
>
> **The End.**
