# Zombie painting production log

The source style reference for every edit was `hellward/assets/painted/mon-zombie.png`. Guides were rendered with `figures.render` into `/tmp/hellward-zombie-anim/`, using `figures.cell('zombie')`, a 254×264 pixel cell, and ground origin `(127, 173.65240157121662)`. `tools/monster_animation.py guide` reproduces the layout and 3:2 padded edit target. The prompts below are the exact text passed to Codex's built-in image generation tool. The final cut sheets are installed as `mon-zombie-bearings`, `mon-zombie-enhanced`, and `mon-zombie-doors`.

## Reusable strip recipe

Render a short guide with `tools/monster_animation.py guide KIND walk|impact|door DIR --facings FACING`. Edit its `*-input.png` with the installed `mon-KIND.png` as a second identity reference. Name the exact row bearings and column actions in the image prompt, including handedness and the final prone silhouette; require the original grid, ground pivot, figure scale, pure `#FF00FF` field, and no floor or effects. Run `cut`, inspect its transparent PNG and cutter diagnostics, then `merge`. A whole painted clip should be installed together, so consecutive poses do not alternate between painting and low-poly guides.

## Fallen

The identity and style reference was the existing `mon-fallen.png`: a small crimson imp with long pointed ears, two short horns, yellow eyes, a ragged brown loincloth, and one short curved blade in its anatomical right hand. The enhanced guide uses a 208×234 pixel cell and origin `(104, 140.8774593472616)`. `mon-fallen-bearings` now contains 48 painted walk poses: eight each for `front_right`, `right`, `back_right`, `back_left`, `left`, and `front_left`. The original painted front/back four-contact cycles remain held into eight slots. All 64 directional hit/death poses and 18 supplemental door attacks are in the other two sheets.

For each complete walking bearing, the final image edit was a 4×2 black-gridded sheet of eight consecutive posed guides. The prompt identified the first image as the exact edit target and `mon-fallen.png` as the painterly identity reference; it required a single Fallen, the stated viewing angle, eight successive leg and arm poses, a low short **curved** right-hand blade, fixed ground pivot and scale, exact grid and margins, and pure flat `#FF00FF` outside each figure. It explicitly excluded raised attack poses, straight staffs, extra weapons, floors, shadows, effects, text, and merged cells. Bearing-specific direction clauses were:

| Facing | Direction clause and visual check |
| --- | --- |
| `front_right` | Diagonally toward camera and screen-right, partial face and chest; knife stays down rather than rising overhead in the walk. |
| `right` | Exactly screen-right in true profile, snout and gaze pointing right. This replaced the old side walk that faced screen-left while registered as right. |
| `back_right` | Diagonally away toward screen-right; back and loincloth visible, at most one partial cheek. |
| `back_left` | Diagonally away toward screen-left; no front-facing eyes. |
| `left` | Exactly screen-left in true profile, snout and gaze pointing left. |
| `front_left` | Diagonally toward camera and screen-left, partial face and chest. |

Each painted output was cut against an eight-cell guide assembled from that bearing's **approved old contact poses**, rather than the low-poly silhouette. That brought the six cycles to the same game-scale footprint and ground point as the existing painting: median heights after registration were approximately 118–121 px for the front and side views and 113–122 px for rear views. All final cuts flagged 0/8 cells and had clear alpha borders. The six complete clips were merged into `mon-fallen-bearings`; the hit/death and door strips were inspected beside their new walk direction. The old right-facing door attack still used a side painting that looked screen-left. A three-cell right-profile wind/strike/recover strip was painted from the new right-walk identity, cut with 0/3 flags, and merged into `mon-fallen-doors`. The first inbetween-only attempt alternated a raised blade, changed a rear-left view into a face, and turned the left profile between steps. A cardinal inbetween draft omitted the blade and shrank relative to the approved front art. These drafts were rejected after 1:1 cycle inspection. The final eight-bearing montage and real-render preview are the visual checks for this selection.

## Skeleton

The identity reference was `hellward/assets/painted/mon-skeleton.png`: yellowed bone, dark rib cavities, red eyes, a rusty straight sword in the anatomical **right** hand, and a worn round wooden shield on the **left** arm. Every guide came from `figures.render('skeleton', facing, frame)` and used a 248×284 pixel cell, ground origin `(124, 182.38785279175812)`, and scale `2`. Edit the magenta, gridded guide with built-in image generation; cut against that same guide with Sagaforge. The installed supplements are `mon-skeleton-bearings` (52 cells), `mon-skeleton-enhanced` (64 cells), and `mon-skeleton-doors` (15 cells).

### Walking bearings

The first 4×5 guide has four contact poses (`walk1`, `walk3`, `walk5`, `walk7`) per row. Its rows are `front_right`, `back_right`, `back_left`, `front_left`, and `left`. Use the original painting as the identity reference and ask for one continuous painted gait in each row. Name all five directions explicitly: a three-quarter face for the front rows, skull/spine and mostly hidden face for the back rows, and a true side profile for `left`. Keep sword and shield attached to their anatomical hands as the occlusion changes. Require the exact grid, ground pivot, pure `#FF00FF` outside the figures, and no shadows, scenery, effects, text, or cell crossings.

For the inbetweens, render a 4×5 guide of `walk2`, `walk4`, `walk6`, `walk8` in those same five rows and a 4×3 guide for `front`, `back`, `right`. Give the painter the existing four painted contact poses for each row. Ask each even pose to fall halfway between its neighboring odd poses, with visible foot, knee, pelvis, and sword-arm changes while keeping scale and the same bearing. These two strips cut with **0/20** and **0/12** flags. A full eight-column cycle montage exposed the existing `back_left` odd row alternating between front and rear views; cutter flags could not detect that. A separate 4×2 repaint of all eight `back_left` steps used the accepted rear-view hit/death sheet as its angle reference and required back of skull, spine, left-arm shield, and right-hand sword in every frame. It cut with **0/8** flags and removed the facing flicker. The final 52 bearing cells have clear alpha borders and were reviewed together at 1:1.

### Hit and death

Paint one 4×2 guide for each of the eight facings. The first row is `hit1`, `hit2`, `hit3`, `death1`; the second is `death2` through `death5`. Use the original painting as the style reference, plus the painted bearing row for a diagonal. Ask for three connected recoil poses, then a fall that separates skull, ribs, limbs, sword, and shield into a low bone pile in the final two cells. Keep the same bearing during the upright poses and retain sword/shield handedness until they drop.

Two generated strips needed explicit repair. `front_right/hit2` lost its sword and the last two deaths retained too much intact rib cage; the edit restored the sword grip and flattened the remains. The first `back_left` strip faced the camera. Regenerating from its procedural guide with the accepted back-right painting as an additional rear-anatomy reference, and explicitly requiring the back of skull, shoulder blades, and spine with **no visible eyes**, produced the accepted rear view. The final eight-direction contact sheet was inspected; all 64 cells have clear alpha borders. Sagaforge flagged pose drift, not clipping, in `front_right/death3` (30.7 px), `back_right/death3` (39.5 px), `back_left/death2` (32.2 px), `back_left/death3` (49.2 px), and `front_left/death3` (40.0 px). Those are the wide falling poses; inspect their ground contact rather than treating centroid drift alone as a failure.

### Door attack

The final 3×5 guide has rows `front_right`, `back_right`, `back_left`, `left`, `front_left` and columns `wind`, `strike`, `recover`. In the edit prompt, describe each row's exact angle and each column's connected action: sword high behind the shoulder, forward lunge and strike, then a lowered guard. Keep both pieces of equipment, the guide's feet, the grid, and flat `#FF00FF`; add no door or target.
